# Figma Support

By creating your account, joining the CMS MX Figma team and enabling the libraries, you will be able to use the Components and Patterns and Figma libraries in your projects.

## Login/ Account Register to Figma

1. Login to [Figma](https://www.figma.com) with `@ha.org.hk` account to access the files.
    - Register a free Figma account if you don't already have one. You can use either a personal email account or a team email account (with internet access), but the email must end with @ha.org.hk.
2. After logging in, look for the `Hospital Authority` in the left column of the Figma interface. Ensure you are on `Hospital Authority` organisation, otherwise you won't be able to use CMS Components

## Getting Access to Our Team Library

1. Download the [CMS MX Templates](https://uiux-doc-cmschassis-dev-st.tstcld61.server.ha.org.hk/docs/guideline/intro/) file.
2. Select `Drafts` and drag the Figma template file to import it. The import process takes approximately 10 to 30 seconds.
3. Once loading is complete, click `Done`. The file will be imported into your Drafts section.
4. Double-click to open the file, select `Assets` panel, click the book icon and then the Manage libraries panel will pop-up.
5. Search for `CMS MX`, and you may find `CMS MX - Components`, `CMS MX - Foundation`, `CMS MX - Icons`
6. Click `Add to file` to add all CMS components in your template file.
7. You can now switch back to the `File` panel to get started with designing your project.
